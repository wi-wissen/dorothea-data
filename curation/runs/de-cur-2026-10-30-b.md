## Why

The engine (Dorothea #62) now reads more prepositional constructions of German verbs, but it
only ever chooses among the card senses. Several meanings had no sense at all (*ums Leben
kommen*, *zu sich kommen*, *nicht dazu kommen*, *an jemanden gehen*, *zu jemandem halten*) or were
hidden or under "more" (*es kommt zu* "to happen", *auf jemanden zählen*, *zu jemandem stehen*;
the heartbeat and the electric shock of *Schlag*; the body shape of *Figur*).
