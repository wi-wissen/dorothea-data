## Why

The engine (Dorothea #84) chooses senses of German homographs from context words, but only among
the card senses. Some meanings were hidden or under "more" (`Schlag` of a clock, `schlagen`
capture and the heartbeat), missing (`Figur` a public figure, `frisch` clean, `treffen` score,
`Schlag` the carriage door), or explained too narrowly in de-en (`ziehen` "move": only moving
house). The English and the German Wiktionary number these senses differently, so an edit of one
direction's id either re-attached in the other by its anchor or was carried there as a second
sense. This run is the first to use the new optional `dirs` list (`docs/FORMAT.md`): a sense edit
or a missing object with `"dirs": ["de-en"]` applies only in de-en.
