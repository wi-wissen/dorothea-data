## Instruction (verbatim)

# Run en-cur-2026-10-21-a: learner-ready English entries (instruction version 3)

You curate English dictionary entries for **Dorothea**, a reading aid for learners and school
students (German speakers learning English, and English readers who want a plain explanation). The
source is English Wiktionary (CC BY-SA). A reader taps a word in a text and sees a small card: it must
show the senses a reader is likely to need, a short explanation in plain English, and correct German
translations. Work from your own knowledge of English and German. Do not consult other dictionaries
or word lists, and do not open any files other than the ones named below.

## Input

`/home/user/dorothea-build/cur-en2/batch-NN.jsonl`, one entry per line: `key, lemma, pos, rank`
(frequency rank, lower = more frequent) and `senses`: `id`, `def` (Wiktionary definition, possibly
cut at 300 characters), `tags` (usage labels), `de` (German translations currently attached; some
were added automatically and may be wrong or belong to another sense). Obsolete, archaic and similar
senses were already removed.

## For every sense decide

1. **`show`**:
   - `main`: a sense a typical reader of everyday, school, news or fiction texts meets, including
     frequent spoken and colloquial uses and common news words. Card limit per **lemma** (all parts
     of speech of the same lemma in your batch together): 6, up to 8 for very versatile words
     (get, take, make, run, set, go, have, put …).
   - `more`: correct and occasionally useful, but secondary. Shown under "More meanings".
   - `hide`: very rare, regional-only, narrow technical or jargon senses, pure cross-references,
     and **duplicates**. Hide a sense as a duplicate only if it means the same as a kept sense; move
     its correct German translations to the kept sense via `de_add`.
2. **`order`**: 1, 2, 3 … over `main` senses first, then `more` senses; most common first.
3. **`learner_en`** (for `main` and `more`): a short explanation in plain English, ≤ 15 words,
   B1 vocabulary where possible. It must describe the **same sense** as the German translations you
   keep. Do not use the headword or a word built from it; no idioms; no parentheses unless needed.
   Write it yourself; do not copy the Wiktionary wording. For function words, describe the use
   ("joins two parts of a sentence when …").
4. **German** (for `main` and `more`, translations are allowed on both):
   - `de_keep`: given translations that are correct for this sense (keep their spelling).
   - `de_drop`: only translations that are **wrong or misleading** for this sense, not correct
     second choices. A string that packs several translations into one item ("laufen, rennen") or
     contains explanations counts as misleading: drop it and add the clean single words.
   - `de_add`: 1–3 common German translations if the sense has none correct, or a clearly better
     everyday one is missing. Nouns with article (`das Haus`), verbs in the infinitive, separable
     verbs written together, base forms only.
5. **`missing`** (entry level, optional): a common meaning no sense covers, as
   `{"learner_en": "…", "de": ["…"]}`.
6. **`see`** (entry level, optional): if all senses are only cross-references, spelling variants or
   inflected forms, the base word they point to (e.g. `"see": "color"`). Hide such senses.
7. **`note`** (optional, ≤ 10 words): why, if you hid a frequent-looking sense.

## Output

`/home/user/dorothea-build/cur-en2/out-NN.jsonl`, one line per input entry, same order:

```json
{"key": "en:run:verb:1", "senses": [{"id": "…", "show": "main", "order": 1, "learner_en": "to move fast on your feet", "de_keep": ["rennen", "laufen"], "de_drop": [], "de_add": []}, {"id": "…", "show": "hide"}], "missing": null, "see": null}
```

Every input sense must appear exactly once with its `id`, in input order. Valid JSON per line.
Verify with a short script before finishing: same keys and sense ids in the same order as the input,
allowed values only, card limit per lemma, `order` puts all `main` before `more`, `learner_en`
≤ 15 words and not containing the headword.
