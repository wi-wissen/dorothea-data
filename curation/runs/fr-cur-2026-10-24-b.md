## Instruction (verbatim)

# Follow-up run: more than one missing meaning per entry

Dorothea is a reading aid. A reader taps a word in a text and sees a card: the shown senses (each with a
short explanation in the headword language and German/English translations) plus entry-level
"missing" senses — everyday meanings or fixed expressions no source sense covers. Until now an entry
could hold only ONE missing sense; now it can hold a list. Curators had to leave out expressions such
as (Spanish) ponerse + adjective, la final, sin querer, en caso de, estar en juego, a fondo, tener en
cuenta, para siempre, a cambio de, en punto, más o menos, por otro lado, en línea; (French) se passer,
tout à fait, à part, avoir lieu.

Your input (one JSON per line): `key, lemma, pos, rank, shown` (the senses already on the card) and
`missing` (the current list). For each entry decide whether a reader of everyday, school or news texts
would tap this word inside a frequent fixed expression, reflexive use or meaning that neither the
shown senses nor the current missing list covers. Only add what is really frequent and useful (most
entries need nothing; at most 3 additions per entry; no duplicates of shown senses; put the expression
on the entry of the word a reader is most likely to tap — usually the noun or verb, not the article or
preposition, unless the expression is built on that function word).

Each missing sense: `{"learner_<lang>": "…", "de": ["…"], "en": ["…"]}` where `<lang>` is the headword
language (es or fr). The explanation: simple language (A2–B1), ≤ 15 words, in the headword language,
naming the expression in the form "(… de)" or describing it, but NEVER containing the headword or a
word built from it. German nouns with article, verbs in the infinitive; English without article,
verbs as "to …". 1–3 translations per language.

Output: `out-<file>.jsonl` next to your input, one line per entry you CHANGE:
`{"key": "<key>", "senses": [], "missing": [<full list: the current missing senses unchanged, then
your additions>], "see": null}` — always the full list, because this run replaces the entry's list.
Validate with a short script (key exists in input, list starts with the current list unchanged,
≤ 15 words, headword not in learner text, JSON valid). Write every decision yourself; scripts only to
assemble and validate. Do not consult other dictionaries.
