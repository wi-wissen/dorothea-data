## Changes

1. *decerno*: la → en started with "to decide by combat". The English Wiktionary sense "to
   decide, decide upon, settle, determine" becomes the first la → en sense as "to decide; to
   resolve; to decree", its near duplicate is hidden, and the `missing` item with the same
   English is repeated so that the build drops it as a repeat in la → en.
2. *secundus* (adj.): the ordinal first in both directions: la → en shows the English sense
   "second" first; la → de puts "zweiter" before "nächster; folgend". The prepositional
   sense ("according to", the preposition `secundum`), a duplicate "propitious" and the
   weather sense are hidden.
3. *ludus*: three English senses that repeat "school" and "public games" (or are rare) hidden
   in la → en, so both directions show game / school / public games.
4. *pone* (adv.): "behind / hinten" first in both directions, "after / hinterher" second,
   "back" as `more`; "in the rear" (a repeat of "behind") hidden.
5. *an*: the bare heading senses "direct" and "indirect" of the English Wiktionary hidden.
6. *duco*: `missing` gets "einen Graben ziehen (fossam ducere)", "eine Mauer anlegen (murum
   ducere)" / "to dig a ditch (fossam ducere)", "to build a wall (murum ducere)" after the
   three earlier items (repeated).
7. *aedes*: temple first ("der Tempel, das Heiligtum" / "temple, shrine"; the packed English
   string dropped), the plural "das Haus (Pl.), die Wohnung (Pl.)" / "house (pl.), dwelling
   (pl.)" second, "das Zimmer, das Gemach" / "room" as `more`; the German Wiktionary's
   explanatory strings dropped, its duplicate and rare senses hidden.
8. *sapiens* (adj.): "weise" / "wise" first, "klug, einsichtig, verständig" / "sensible,
   judicious" second, `missing` "der weise Mann, der Weise" / "wise man, sage". The English on
   the German Wiktionary senses gives la → en the entry it lacked.
